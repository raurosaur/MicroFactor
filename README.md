# MICROFactor

## The holistic meal diary

Microfactor is a food tracking app that tries to focus on a more holistic approach to nutrition. Traditional calories/macro tracking apps heavily focus on meeting the macro goals of protein, carbs and fats. But here we often miss out on the needs of our own bodies. The Fibres, the Zincs, the Vitamin Bs, all are essential for the body to function to its optimal. MicroFit focuses on both Macronutrients and Micronutrients. Every day is a diary log that gives a score with a heavier weightage to the micronutrients.

## Current Features

- Add meals to diary
- View Scores and each item everyday
- Store upto 7days of date
